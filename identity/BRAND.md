## Name rationale

**pebble** — light, relatable, animate (the Energy fleet naming family, D4/D5). A pebble is the
smallest unit you actually ship and hold in your hand; you skip it across water. That *is* a mobile
app — the small thing in your pocket, the thing you carry. Symbolic connection to the workflow:
pebble takes a product idea and reduces it to the one small, shippable, pocketable artifact — a
native iOS + Android app — and nothing more (no web, no backend, no payments; those are siblings).

Locked name (DECISIONS D5). Sibling family: **pixel** (frontend) · pebble (mobile) ·
**turbo** (backend) · **acorn** (database) · **badge** (auth) · **penny** (payments) ·
**rocket** (deploy + CI).
